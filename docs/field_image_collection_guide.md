# Field Image Collection Guide

## Required classes

1. crt
2. lcd_panel
3. pcb
4. cable
5. battery
6. motor
7. magnet_bearing_assembly
8. mixed_plastics

## Photo rules

- Use real scrap/e-waste items.
- Capture the object as it normally appears at the collection point.
- Take multiple angles when practical.
- Include different lighting and backgrounds.
- Avoid intentionally clean studio-style arrangements.
- Do not use screenshots or downloaded internet images.
- Do not include faces, phone numbers, addresses, or other personal information.
- Keep the original image files unchanged.

## Per-item target

Aim for at least 20-30 images per class initially.

For each item, vary:
- angle
- distance
- orientation
- lighting
- background
- amount of surrounding scrap

## Important class boundaries

crt:
- Old CRT monitor/television display units.
- Do not label flat-panel LCD/LED displays as CRT.

lcd_panel:
- Actual LCD display/panel components.
- Do not label a generic television/monitor as LCD unless the LCD panel is visually confirmed.

pcb:
- Actual printed circuit boards.

cable:
- Scrap electrical/electronic cables or wires.

battery:
- Actual batteries or battery packs.

motor:
- Actual electric motors.

magnet_bearing_assembly:
- Assemblies where the magnet/bearing structure is visibly identifiable.
- Do not automatically label an ordinary motor or metal part as this class.

mixed_plastics:
- Mixed plastic scrap associated with e-waste/recycling.
- Do not use ordinary household plastic unless it represents the intended scrap category.
